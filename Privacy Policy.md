## Game Claim Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Game Claim is a browser extension developed by OddBoxCode. It displays free game giveaways and provides optional desktop notifications and toolbar indicators.

## Information stored on your device

Game Claim stores the following information locally in your browser:

- Preferences, including enabled stores, notifications, icon animations, sorting, and window sizes.
- Games you mark as "Seen it" (claimed or not interested).
- Cached giveaway listings, game ratings, prices, and identifiers of giveaways you have already been notified about.
- Update timestamps and operational error messages.

This information supports the extension's features, preserves your choices, and helps prevent duplicate notifications. Game Claim does not transmit these locally saved preferences or "Seen it" marks to the developer.

## Connections to external services

Game Claim retrieves giveaway information from GamerPower and game information from Steam. Requests to Steam include game titles used to find matching games, ratings, review counts, and prices. The extension also loads game images from addresses provided in giveaway listings.

These connections expose normal network information, such as your IP address, to the services receiving the requests. Those services may process requests according to their own privacy policies.

When you click a giveaway, a notification, or another link in the extension, Game Claim opens the relevant website or your email app. Your use of that website is subject to its own privacy policy.

## How information is used

Game Claim uses information only to display and update giveaway listings, show ratings and prices, apply your preferences, remember games you have marked "Seen it", and provide notifications and toolbar indicators.

Game Claim does not include analytics or advertising trackers, monitor your browsing history, or collect passwords, payment details, or personal communications.

## Sharing and selling information

OddBoxCode does not sell user data or transfer locally saved user choices to third parties. External requests described above are used only to provide the extension's features.

User data is not used for unrelated purposes, creditworthiness assessments, or lending.

## Storage and deletion

Preferences and saved choices remain in local extension storage until changed, cleared, or removed. Cached information is refreshed periodically, and outdated records are removed automatically.

You can change preferences and undo "Seen it" marks within the extension. Uninstalling Game Claim removes its local extension storage from that browser profile.

## Security

Game Claim uses HTTPS when requesting information from GamerPower and Steam. Locally saved information is held in the browser's extension storage.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the extension's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
